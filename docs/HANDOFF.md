## TL;DR

The island is gone. The whole world is now **Naman's World Fair** — a flat, fenced 72×56 fairground you walk into through a gate. **Bo** runs the ticket booth: three lines, the **About** card, then "tickets are one word each" — the **Wordle** *is* the entry ticket, and until you win it two turnstiles physically block the gate. Inside, the résumé is the midway. The **Career Coaster** is ridden for real: the player hides, a cart follows a drawn 3-span track with a lift hill, drops, a camelback and a loop while the camera follows it, and **five content-derived milestone cards** slide in at five stops (2020 SRM · 2023 Barclays intern · 2024 SDE · the payment-lineage engine in production · the unnamed product now) before the **Career** card lands Education + Experience together. The stalls pay the rest: **claw** at the Prize Tent (one project card per prize), **Word Forge** (now persistent, with a prize board and a cumulative **tech-stack** popup), **Chalk Flight** and the **Arcade** for fun and hats, **Hook-a-Duck** at the pond (Byte the cat), and the **Guestbook** for Contact. Every game carries a visible **"show me the answers"** door — the fair is a résumé, and a résumé you have to be good at Wordle to read is not a résumé. Story spine `ticket → ride → prizes → toolkit → guestbook`; **7 quests / 23 achievements / 8 attractions**; save schema **v4**. **66 files / 1,105 tests / 0 skips**, tsc clean, build green. **Live** at naman-gururani.github.io/lineage since 2026-09-03.

## Conventions & critical gotchas

1. **Never commit/push/deploy unless the user asks.** v4 is committed on `v3/story-isle` (see the header); the branch has never been pushed to `origin` — only gh-pages was published, by `npx gh-pages -d dist`. Commit trailers: `Co-Authored-By: Claude Fable 5.1 <noreply@anthropic.com>` + `Claude-Session: <url>`.
2. **Content rules:** facts about Naman only from `src/data/content.ts`; dialogue carries **no digits** and no facts (cards do); skills = approved set only; the in-development product stays unnamed. Wordle answers, Forge words **and every coaster stop string** are derived from / drift-tested against content. `index.html`'s description + noscript block are pinned to content too (`tests/index-html.test.ts`).
3. **Keyboard:** world input via `src/core/keys.ts` only; `ui/*` never imports it (module-graph test). Mini-game renderers attach their own listeners to the modal root with `e.repeat` guards.
4. **Footprint drawing (v4):** `WorldScene.drawFootprint()` draws **both** structures and attractions — origin `(0.5, 1)`, i.e. centred on the footprint standing on its bottom edge, depth = that bottom-edge y. `buildStructures()` runs **before** `buildAttractions()`, and they share the same depth scheme, so overlap is resolved by y alone (the station sits flush at the spans' foot on purpose). A sprite taller or wider than its plot overhangs — that is deliberate for the coaster, the wheel and the pond stall.
5. **Solids come from three helpers, not from the art:** `boundarySolids(bp)` = the hard full-tile perimeter ring (the decorative fence/bush props are separate and stay **low**, so hopping still works over decor but never over the boundary); `structureSolids(bp, flags)` = the structures, gated ones only while their flag is unset; `attractionSolids(a)` = the footprint, or `solidCols` for the gate arch's two pillars. Never infer a solid from a sprite's rectangle.
6. **The interact radius is small — 26 px** on attraction doors (28 on the forge board, which was widened for exactly this reason). A walk that drifts a tile off the door gets no prompt. When scripting a browser walk, aim at the **door tile centre**, and check `window.__game.scene.getScene('world').interact.current` to see what is actually in range before pressing the key. Doors sit at `priority: 4`, one rung above NPC talk (3): on a door tile the door wins even with a keeper standing on the step (Ilse used to swallow the guestbook's E); a step to the side and you talk to them.
7. **Night overlays** are `${sprite}_night` images at `NIGHT_GLOW_DEPTH = 91000` — **above** `DayNight`'s veil (depth 90000) and deliberately **outside** `tintables`, or the lit coaster gets graded down by the very darkness it exists to break.
8. **Known latent trap (backlog, not a v4 regression):** NPC, player and companion sprites are pushed into `tintables` from `buildNpcs()`, which runs *after* `registerTinted` has already spread its argument — so those registrations never reach `DayNight`. They are untinted at night, which is accidentally why the coaster cart looks right. Fixing the registration would make the cart the one untinted thing on screen; fix both or neither.
9. **Purity:** `core/`, `world/`, `src/games/*`, `src/data/*` and `systems/ridepath.ts` never import Phaser or DOM (exception: `core/events.ts`). Games are pure reducers; renderers are thin DOM/canvas; simulation is driven only by `createLoop`, never timers.
10. **Minigame framework:** rewards/records happen ONLY in `MinigameHost.teardown()`; chapters unlock through `GameState.unlockFacet(zoneId, announce)` — the claw calls it with `announce=false` *before* opening the card itself; the panel layer's `facet:unlocked` listener pushes into `uiState.unlocked` **before** deciding whether to queue a card. Renderers that can outlive their win expose `won()`. `host.progress(data)` → `GameState.minigameProgress(id, data)` is the persistence seam; `WorldScene.openPanel()` is the **only** place that attaches the forge payload, so every route to the board is fed.
11. **Story wiring:** Bo's `intro` node is not in his entry ladder — only the fresh-save arrival runs it, and it unlocks `about` before the card effect flushes. `uiState.unlocked` is a **copy** of the save array. Bo relocates between `storySpots` only **off-camera**. `objectiveFor()` returns `null` once `story_done` is set, whatever the step says — that is what hides the HUD chip and the map marker after the finale.
12. **Art pipeline:** 32 px/tile; palette keys only; new packs register in `src/art/atlas.ts` (`allDefs()` now spreads FAIR_DEFS and RIDE_DEFS, and `tests/atlas-capacity.test.ts` pins that list); per-pack tests pin dims/anchors; render `npm run preview:art -- sheet <pack> 3` and look. Style rules: `.superpowers/sdd/2026-09-01-naman-world-hd/art-direction.md`.
13. **Browser testing protocol:** the dev server on `:5173` hot-reloads on every source change — with agents editing concurrently, **build and test against `npx vite preview --port 4173` instead**. URL flags: `?fresh=1`, `?cheat=1` (Skip in every game), `?word=<answer>`, `?duck=gold`, `?st=1` (timer stepping; distorts feel). Synthetic window `KeyboardEvent`s reach `core/keys` consumers but **not** element-scoped listeners — use real CDP keys/clicks, and **hold the world's action key ~120 ms**; a shorter tap is routinely missed. A gag modal intercepts pointer clicks (use `document.querySelector(...).click()` from `browser_evaluate`). `window.__game` / `window.__events` are exposed; `__game.scene.getScene('world')` gives `.state` (live GameState) and `.interact`; `__events.emit('ui:panel', {id:'minigame', data:'<id>'})` opens a game directly.
14. **Windows shell:** long heredocs get mangled — write `scratch/*.cjs` with the Write tool. **Playwright MCP's browser profile is shared across sessions** and refuses a second user ("Browser is already in use"); when that happens, drive `playwright-core` from the MCP's npx cache directly (`C:/Users/Naman/AppData/Local/npm-cache/_npx/<hash>/node_modules/playwright-core`, `chromium.launch({ channel: 'chrome', headless: true })`) — `scratch/recheck*.cjs` are working examples (fresh save → Bo → ticket → ride → games → finale, with `window.__game` probes).
15. **Registry invariant:** every zone is delivered by exactly one attraction, the 8 attraction ids are the discoverable set, and `DISCOVERIES_FOR_100` is 8 (`tests/registry.test.ts`).
16. **Parallel agents:** disjoint file ownership per task, one CSS file per game (`src/styles/games/<id>.css`), review packages were built from working-tree snapshots (`scratch/snap.sh`, `scratch/pkg.sh`) while the work was uncommitted; from here on plain `git diff <base>` does the same job.
